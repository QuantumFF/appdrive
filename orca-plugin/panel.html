appdrive

Agents drive your apps on a hidden display with appdrive: your pointer, focus and monitors stay yours.

Each session opens its live dashboard as a browser tab in its worktree, and the worktree card shows the last action.

Commands (command palette):

  • appdrive: Open this worktree's dashboard
  • appdrive: Open every running dashboard
  • appdrive: Show running sessions

You get a notification when a session crashes or fails to launch. This panel is static: Orca panels cannot load live data.
